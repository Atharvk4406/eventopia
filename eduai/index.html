Our Mission — SDG 4: Quality Education

Bridging the education gap with artificial intelligence

🌍

The Problem

Over 300 million students worldwide lack access to qualified tutors. Personalised learning remains a privilege of the few.

🤖

Our AI Solution

EduAI uses Google Gemini to provide every student with a 24/7 personalised tutor, quiz generator, and study planner — for free.

📈

The Impact

Students can self-study effectively at their own pace, reducing dependence on costly private tuition and improving learning outcomes.
